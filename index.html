A hoodtrap beat plays while you fly, made live in your browser. It gets heavier in boss fights. Boss fights play ELECTROSOULS. You can swap in your own audio files (MP3, M4A, WAV or OGG): pick one or several songs for flying (they play one after another) and one for boss fights. Open the track on SoundCloud · or on YouTube

Click to grab the mouse, then move it to turn the rocket: you can turn all the way round and fly in any direction. Hold Space, W or the mouse button to thrust along the nose, S for retro jets, A D and the arrow keys to turn, Q E to strafe down and up, Shift to warp, C to change camera. Altitude only counts when you fly forward: if you turn away, an arrow points back to the climb. Boss fights fly the same way: turn and thrust to dodge, and an arrow points to the boss when it is off screen. The edge of space is a wall of rock: you bounce off it. Bosses fire lasers: watch for the red target ring and get out of it. On a phone, touch and hold where you want to fly.
